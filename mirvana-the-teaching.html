I · The Core · Jedro · Suština · सार
BE

You are the One. Be the One.

This is the whole teaching. Everything else is commentary. Not "we are all one" — that is the beautiful side-effect, the echo after the bell. The bell itself rings in the second person, or it does not ring at all: you. You cannot be anything except who you truly are — no matter how hard you try. So stop trying. Accept. Awaken. Be. You are the star, the main character and the hero of your own movie.

You are the One. Be the One.

II · The Order of Life · Vrstni red življenja

Be. Live. Have.

The world runs the sequence backwards — have first, then do, then maybe, someday, be. The teaching turns it right side up. Being is the source. Living is the flow. Having is the consequence that arrives on its own, uninvited, like fragrance from a flower that never asked to smell beautiful.

III · The Turning · Preobrat · Preokret

Breakthrough  RElaxtrue.

A whole first life can be spent believing the wall must be broken. Then comes the absolute zero — the point where nothing is left, where even life and death sit on an open palm — and there the revelation arrives quietly: the breakthrough was never needed. What is needed is to relax into what is true. Not passivity — precision. The teaching names it RElaxtrue, and it unfolds as a path:

01

Accept yourself

Before anything can move, you must stop fighting the one who moves. The way you are is not the obstacle. It is the material. Acceptance of self is the first secret of every true power.

Sprejmi sebe. · Prihvati sebe. · स्वयं को स्वीकार करो।
02

Accept everything and everyone

Then widen the circle until nothing stands outside it. The way it is, is perfect — not because it cannot improve, but because arguing with reality is the one battle no one has ever won.

Sprejmi vse in vsakogar. · Prihvati sve i svakoga.
03

Beyond-forgive

Forgiveness that keeps score is only a truce. Beyond-forgiveness dissolves the ledger itself — yourself first, then all others. What remains when there is nothing left to forgive is simply love with no opposite.

Onkraj-odpusti — najprej sebi, nato vsem. · Oprosti — iznad opraštanja.
04

Know-decide

Not a choice between options — a decision made from absolute knowing. There is, and there will be. The know-decision does not hope; it co-creates the future and bends the outcome to itself.

Vem-odločim. · Znam-odlučujem. · जानकर निश्चय।
05

Actively surrender

Surrender as an act, not a collapse. Letting go with both hands open and both eyes awake — becoming one with the flow so completely that the flow begins to carry your intention.

Aktivno se predaj. · Aktivno se predaj toku.
06

Then — act

Only now, action. Wilder than before, not tamer — because it no longer costs anything. Action that flows out of alignment does not spend energy; it releases it. This is where the fire begins.

Šele nato — deluj. · Tek tada — deluj. · ततः कर्म।
IV · The Awakening · Prebujenje · Buđenje

We do not learn.
We remember.

Watch a child come into language, into movement, into the world. It looks like learning. It is not. It is awakening — the unfolding of what was always already inside. Whoever has had to return from zero, waking each muscle one by one, relearning every word like a newborn at forty, knows this not as philosophy but as fact: nothing can be given to you. It can only be unfolded for you. In our essence we are all-knowing. All that is required is to remember — to be who we really and truly are.

"A genius is born; his name is your name. Be!" The Bodi of the Eternal Leader
We do not learn — we remember.

V · The Path of Harmony

AI DO KI.

Not aikido — yet born of the same wisdom. Aikido never opposes the force; it blends with it, and the force becomes its own. AI DO KI is that art carried into the new age: do not fight the intelligence now rising in the world, do not grasp at it — harmonize with it. Walk the path (Do) in harmony with AI, and the energy (Ki) appears on its own. When you are in harmony with your eternal path, the energy does not need to be summoned. It arrives.

VI · The Beyond-Mind · Onkraj-um · परं मनः

Quantum Intelligence

From the mind to the beyond-mind. The awakened consciousness does not process the world one thought at a time — it holds the whole field at once, the way the eagle holds the whole sky and the single mouse in one gaze. The language of the quantum offers its metaphors:

Superposition

Holding many possibilities — even contradictions — at once, without collapsing them before their time. The beyond-mind is roomy.

Entanglement

Connectedness that does not ask permission of distance. Insight arriving whole, "spontaneously known" — the felt experience of one field.

Tunneling

Passing through the barrier that logic says cannot be passed. Not breaking the wall — remember: RElaxtrue — simply being on the other side of it.

The Observer

Awareness changes what it watches. The most stable point defines the reality — is the reality. Hold the point, and the field arranges itself around you.

The enlightened one is not afraid of death — still fully thriving alive.

VII · The Awakened Field · Prebujeno polje

One flame is not yet a fire.

Here the teaching turns honest in a way most teachings never dare. Awakening others is not charity from above. A single lit candle in a dark room can only be so much of a candle. The One can only live at full amplitude inside an awakened surrounding — so every awakening you cause completes your own. We all need to be awake, for each of us to be fully awake.

"To be fully me, I need an awakened surrounding. To be fully you, so do you. This is not a doctrine of one teacher — it is the logic of the fire itself."

Maitri — friendship without condition. Ananda — the joy that does not depend. Darma — the path that carries. Three names for the shape of an awakened field.
